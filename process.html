Reading the precedence columns

Position comes from the arrows rather than from stages invented for the diagram, and the merged headers above say which stage each span belongs to.

  • Steps in the first column have nothing before them. That does not mean they happen first in time; it means the data records no earlier step.
  • A step can sit further right than its stage suggests when more steps must come before it. Switch to “By stage” to compare the two views.
